About

I am Meiqi Zhang, a Financial Mathematics & Statistics graduate from the University of Sydney (GPA 4.0, Dalyell Scholar), and an incoming M.Sc. Computer Science student at the University of Hong Kong (Delta X Tech Immersion Fellowship).

My work sits at the intersection of mathematical optimization, stochastic programming, and quantitative finance — from cardinality-constrained portfolio MIQP solved via Lagrangian relaxation, to two-stage stochastic programs solved with the L-shaped method, to LLM-guided reinforcement learning for portfolio management. I am actively seeking graduate study and research opportunities in operations research and quantitative modeling.
