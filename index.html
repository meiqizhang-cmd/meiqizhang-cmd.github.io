Optimization Projects

Large-Scale Portfolio Optimization: Cardinality-Constrained MIQP & Lagrangian Relaxation

Feb – Apr 2026

Extends Markowitz mean-variance optimization to the NP-hard cardinality-constrained setting (hold ≤ K assets): exact MIQP via Gurobi, and Lagrangian relaxation with Polyak subgradient updates for large universes — validated on S&P 500 constituent data.

  • Lagrangian decomposition: convex QP x-subproblem (CVXPY/OSQP) + closed-form greedy z-subproblem; <2% optimality gap at a fraction of MIQP solve time.
  • Scalability study N = 50 → 500, rolling out-of-sample backtest, Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage, and a robust-optimization extension with ellipsoidal uncertainty sets.

Two-Stage Stochastic Portfolio Optimization via the L-Shaped Method

Apr – Jun 2026

Two-stage stochastic program (SAA over 500 bootstrap scenarios from real ETF data) with CVaR risk linearized per Rockafellar-Uryasev; solved via the L-shaped (Benders) method and progressive hedging.

  • L-shaped master accumulates optimality cuts from stage-2 LP duals; single-cut and multi-cut variants with certified UB/LB convergence.
  • Quantified VSS and EVPI against expected-value and wait-and-see benchmarks; out-of-sample backtest shows the SP portfolio dominating the EV portfolio on CVaR and Sharpe.
